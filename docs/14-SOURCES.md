## S40: VChatter exploratory study

[Open source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.03520)

Zhang, Tsang, and Peng; submitted June 4, 2025. Abstract reviewed October 3, 2026. Describes generative conversational roleplay informed by a survey (36 respondents) and an expert interview, followed by a six-day qualitative study (10 participants). Preliminary evidence of feasibility and user experience; not a randomized efficacy trial or evidence that this app treats social anxiety. Does not establish a best demo scenario.

## How to interpret the recommendation

The project is feasible as a tightly scoped hackathon prototype. Differentiation and commercial demand remain hypotheses. A clinician-informed concept is not evidence of treatment efficacy. The session limits and reflection rules in this pack are product choices requiring evaluation.

GitHub popularity was not used as a quality score. No repository code audit was performed. Prefer a few reviewed integrations, pin installed versions after validation, and keep app runtime dependencies separate from Codex tooling.
