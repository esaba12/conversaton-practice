THE EXPERIENCE WE’RE BUILDING

From “how do I say this?”
to giving it a try.

01

Bring your situation.

Asking for help. Setting a boundary. Saying no. Start with a conversation from your own everyday life.

02

Make it feel familiar.

Review and edit a fictional counterpart’s role and communication style. Choose the pace and challenge that work for you.

03

Say it out loud.

Practice in a live voice exchange, then take a moment to reflect. Finish when you’re ready.

BUILT AROUND YOUR CONTROL

Practice, without
the pressure to perform.

We’re designing for fresh starts, settings you can edit, and memory you explicitly approve.

No social scores. No predictions about real people. Just a bounded space to try a conversation before taking it into your life.

A rehearsal tool, not therapy or medical advice.
